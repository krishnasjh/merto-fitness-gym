Our Story

Kanpur's Trusted Fitness Home

Metro Fitness Gym has been a pillar of the Kanpur fitness community for over a decade. Founded with the mission to make world-class fitness accessible to every local resident, we've helped hundreds of members transform their health, confidence, and lifestyle.

Located conveniently on the Kanpur–Allahabad Highway in Ayodhya Vihar, we are easily accessible from Chakeri and surrounding areas. Our state-of-the-art facility features premium equipment, spacious workout areas, and a team of passionate, certified trainers who are genuinely invested in your success.

Our Story

Your Friendly Neighbourhood Fitness Hub in Kanpur

Metro Fitness Gym started with a simple belief — that quality fitness should be accessible to everyone in Kanpur, regardless of age, budget, or fitness level. Over the past decade, we've grown from a small local gym to Kanpur's friendliest training community.

Conveniently located on the Kanpur–Allahabad Highway in Ayodhya Vihar, Chakeri, we serve thousands of members from across Kanpur who trust us for our welcoming environment, qualified trainers, modern equipment, and genuinely affordable plans. Come visit us — we'd love to be part of your fitness journey!

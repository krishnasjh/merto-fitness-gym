Our Story

Built in Kanpur.
Built for Champions.

Metro Fitness Gym was born from a singular obsession: to create the most serious, results-driven training environment in Kanpur. For over a decade, we have been the go-to destination for athletes, working professionals, and fitness enthusiasts who demand more from their gym.

Located strategically on the Kanpur–Allahabad Highway in Chakeri, our facility is easily reachable and features cutting-edge equipment, a massive training floor, dedicated cardio zones, and a team of India's finest certified coaches who are relentlessly committed to your success.
